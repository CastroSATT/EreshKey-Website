The Name

Inspired by ancient guardians of the underworld

👑

EreshKey: Guardian of Your Secrets

The name EreshKey draws inspiration from Ereshkigal, the ancient Mesopotamian goddess of the underworld. In mythology, Ereshkigal was the powerful guardian of the realm of the dead, protecting what was most sacred and valuable.

Just as Ereshkigal guarded the underworld with absolute authority, EreshKey guards your digital secrets with the same unwavering protection. Your passwords, notes, and sensitive data are kept secure in the immutable realm of the blockchain—protected, permanent, and under your complete control.

From ancient guardians to modern cryptography—your keys, your sovereignty.
